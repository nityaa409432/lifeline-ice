    Make sure it shows when the phone is locked

    Android: Settings, Notifications, Notifications on lock screen, choose "Show all content" (some phones call it "Sensitive notifications"). Press and hold the Lifeline card and set it to Default or Priority, not Silent.

    iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later): in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Lifeline from the new icon and press Pin. Then Settings, Notifications, Lifeline, turn on Lock Screen and set Show Previews to Always. iPhones move older notifications into Notification Center, so open Lifeline once a day to bring the card back to the top.

    Swiped it away by mistake? Open Lifeline and it pins itself again.
